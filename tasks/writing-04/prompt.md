# Task: Write a Difficult Performance Feedback Email

## Situation

You are an engineering manager. One of your senior engineers, **Alex**, has been underperforming for the past quarter. Specifically:

1. **Missed two project deadlines** — the Persona V3 integration was due March 15 and shipped March 28 (13 days late). The evaluation pipeline was due April 1 and is still not complete.
2. **Code review quality declined** — other team members have complained that Alex's reviews are superficial (one-line "LGTM" on complex PRs).
3. **Communication gaps** — Alex missed two team standups without notice and was unresponsive on Teams for 3+ hours on multiple occasions.

However, Alex was a **top performer last year** — led the Persona V2 launch, mentored two junior engineers, and received a "Exceeds" rating.

## Assignment

Write an email to Alex scheduling a 1:1 to discuss these concerns. The email should:

1. **Not blindside** — Alex should know the meeting is about performance, not be surprised in the room
2. **Be specific** — reference the concrete examples above
3. **Be empathetic** — acknowledge Alex's track record and that something may have changed
4. **Be actionable** — set clear expectations for what you want to discuss
5. **Maintain trust** — this is a conversation, not a punishment. The goal is support and course-correction.
6. **Professional tone** — would survive being forwarded to HR or Alex's skip-level

## Constraints
- Subject line included
- 200-350 words (concise — managers write too much)
- No corporate jargon ("Let's align on synergies") 
- No passive-aggressive language ("As I'm sure you're aware...")
