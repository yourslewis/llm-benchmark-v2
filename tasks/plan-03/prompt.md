# Task: Draft a Reimbursement Email for a Lost Restaurant Receipt

## Situation

You need to submit a reimbursement request for a business meal, but you lost the paper receipt. You need to write a professional email to the restaurant requesting a copy or confirmation.

## Details

- **Restaurant name:** Boardwalk Restaurant
- **Email address:** bwhost@microsoft.com
- **Date of visit:** March 12, 2026
- **Time:** 1:58 PM
- **Amount charged:** $515.56
- **Payment method:** American Express card (last 4 digits unknown, Amex ending in ••••)
- **Purpose:** Business lunch (you were hosting a client meeting)
- **Your name:** Alex Chen
- **Your company:** Contoso Technologies

## What the Email Needs to Include

1. **Clear subject line** that makes the purpose obvious
2. **Polite opening** — professional but not stiff
3. **Specific transaction details** so they can locate the record:
   - Date and time of visit
   - Approximate amount
   - Payment method
4. **The ask** — request a copy of the receipt or a written confirmation of the transaction for reimbursement purposes
5. **Deadline context** — mention that you need this for expense report submission (end of month)
6. **Contact info** — your email and phone so they can reach you
7. **Professional closing**

## Tone

Professional, polite, and concise. Not overly formal. Should read like a real person wrote it, not a template.

## Output Format

Write the complete email, ready to send. Include:
- Subject line
- Body
- Signature block with name, title (Account Manager), company, email, and phone number (555-0147)
